# First-Screen Intent

Status: proposed for repository review. Source: [product baseline](../reference/product-baseline.md).

Deliver a simple, pleasant, useful first screen that works on a phone and remains useful offline.

## Intended outcomes

- Scan the whole life map.
- Expand categories without losing surrounding context.
- Distinguish actual condition from action progress.
- Recognize the business day currently being reviewed.
- Access previously loaded information without connectivity.
- Provide a manageable opportunity for hands-on contemporary React development.

## Boundaries

Build the agreed category-and-lucket display, persisted data foundation, and offline foundation.
Preserve taxonomy and names.
Additional views, automatic source synchronization, AI automation, native packaging, and broader collaboration are excluded.
Editing and day closing depend on unresolved interactions and history/conflict decisions; do not enable them by inference.
See the [roadmap](../07-planning/roadmap.md) and [decision register](../07-planning/open-decisions.md).

Success means usefulness during ordinary personal review, not making every indicator green.
Maintenance, deliberate inactivity, and nothing required are valid outcomes.
